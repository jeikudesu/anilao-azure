02 / OVERVIEW

The Birthplace of
Scuba Diving in PH

Located in the coastal municipality of Mabini, Batangas, Anilao is celebrated globally as a premier macro diving sanctuary. Just a few hours from Manila, it offers crystal-clear waters, vibrant coral walls, and incredible marine biodiversity suitable for both novice divers and experienced underwater photographers.

Suggested Rides from Manila

OPTION A

🚗 Private Route

Take SLEX to STAR Tollway, exit at Batangas City Grand Terminal, head through Bauan, and follow the scenic coastal road straight to Mabini.

OPTION B

🚌 Public Transit

Board a Batangas City bus from Cubao or Buendia. From the grand terminal, ride a Mabini jeepney and hire a tricycle to your resort.

04 / KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best season to visit Anilao for diving? ▼

November to May offers the dry season with maximum water clarity, smooth ocean conditions, and optimal visibility for macro photography.

Is a scuba license mandatory for first-timers? ▼

No! Beginners can enroll in 'Discover Scuba Diving' programs hosted by certified instructors across Anilao resorts.

Does Anilao have sandy white beaches? ▼

Anilao is primarily characterized by rocky, pebble coastlines. This prevents sand churn, leaving the water pristine for coral ecosystems.

What marine species can I expect to see? ▼

Anilao is the world capital for nudibranchs, blue-ringed octopuses, frogfish, pygmy seahorses, and vibrant hard and soft coral gardens.
